RECIPE OF BIRYANI

Biryani is a flavorful mixed rice dish from the Indian subcontinent, made with spices, rice, and meat, seafood, or vegetables, with origins in Mughal-era India, possibly derived from Persian rice dishes.

PREPARATION TIME

  • Ingredient Preparation:20 to 25 minutes
  • Marination:1 to 2 hours
  • Cooking & Assembling:45 to 60 minutes

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 kg (2.2 lbs) chicken (bone-in), beef, or mutton, cut into pieces.
  • 3/4 cup yogurt, 2 tbsp ginger paste, 2 tbsp garlic paste, and 2 medium onions (finely sliced for frying).
  • 3-4 tomatoes (diced) and 2 medium potatoes, peeled and halved.
  • 2-3 tsp salt, 1 tbsp red chili powder, 1/2tsp turmeric, and 2-3 tbsp biryani masala (or a mix of cumin, coriander, and garam masala).
  • 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves, \(\frac{1}{2}\) cup fresh coriander (cilantro), and 4-6 slit green chilies.
  • 750g (3.5 cups) aged, long-grain Basmati rice (washed and soaked for 30 minutes).
  • 5 green cardamoms, 4-5 cloves, 1-2 black cardamoms, 1-2 star anise, 2 bay leaves, and 1 cinnamon stick.
  • 4-5 liters of water with 3 tbsp salt and 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar to keep the grains separated.
  • 1/2 cup, used for frying onions and for drizzling over the layers.
  • A few drops of kewra water (pandanus extract) and 1 tsp saffron soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk.

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. In a bowl, combine the chicken with the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, spice powders, and salt. Let it marinate for at least 1 hour in the fridge.
  2. Heat oil/ghee in a large pot. Add the sliced onions and fry until golden brown. Remove half of the fried onions for later garnishing. Add whole spices to the remaining onions and sauté for 1 minute.
  3. Add the marinated chicken to the pot. Sauté for 5 minutes on medium-high heat, then lower the heat, cover, and cook until the chicken is tender and oil separates (about 15-20 minutes).
  4. In a separate, large pot, boil 2.5 liters of water with 2.5 tablespoons of salt and a few whole spices (cardamom, cloves, cinnamon). Add the soaked rice and cook for about 5to 7 minutes until it is 70% cooked (it should have a slight bite in the middle). Drain completely.
  5. In the same pot with the chicken, layer half of the semi-cooked rice on top. Sprinkle with mint, coriander, the saved fried onions, and a dash of biryani masala. Add the remaining rice on top, drizzle with the saffron-infused milk and a tablespoon of ghee.
  6. Cover the pot tightly with a heavy lid (you can seal the edges with dough or a clean kitchen towel to trap the steam). Place the pot on low heat and cook for 15 to 20 minutes.

NUTRITIONS

Biryani is a nutrient-dense, carb-and-protein-heavy dish.
